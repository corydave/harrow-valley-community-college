About this site and credits

Harrow Valley Community College isn't real. Its people, phone numbers, addresses, policies, and documents are invented. Phone numbers use the fictional 555-01xx range, and email addresses end in .example, which can never belong to a real site.

Why it exists

This site was built as a teaching artifact for The Neuroaccessible Environment, a workshop at Finger Lakes Community College on using AI to design for sensory, executive function, and communication diversity. The practice documents in the workshop all come from Harrow Valley, and here they are in the places a real college would post them.
