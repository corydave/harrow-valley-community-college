Campus Safety

Campus Safety keeps the campus safe and issues visitor credentials.

Emergency? Call 911. Then call the 24-hour Campus Safety line at (555) 555-0100, ext. 4444.

Campus Safety Office

Where
Building B, Room B-114 (next to Lot 4)
Phone
(555) 555-0100, ext. 4417
Email
campussafety@harrowvalley.example
Posted hours
Monday to Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Officers are on campus 24 hours a day.
Director
Director of Campus Safety, Facilities, and Risk Management

What we do

  • Issue temporary visitor credentials (TVCs) and extended visitor registrations
  • Patrol buildings and parking lots
  • Walk anyone to their car after dark: call ext. 4444
  • Lost and found
  • Jump-starts and lockouts in campus lots

Visitor credentials

Most visitors need a temporary visitor credential (TVC) to park. Read the visitor parking procedures, then request a TVC online or stop by Room B-114.
