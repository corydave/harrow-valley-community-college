Visit & Parking

Everything you need before you come to campus.

Parking comes first

All visitors who park on campus during regular operating hours need a temporary visitor credential, except in the short-term visitor spaces in Lot 4.

Read the visitor parking procedures Get a visitor credential

Getting here

Address
1200 Valley Ridge Road, Harrow, New York
Main entrance
Valley Ridge Road, at the traffic light. Follow the green "Visitors" signs to Lot 4.
Bus
Valley Transit routes 3 and 12 stop at the Student Center loop every 30 minutes on weekdays.
