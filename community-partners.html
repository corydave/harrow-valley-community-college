Community partners

Local agencies that work with Harrow Valley students and neighbors.

Harrow Valley works with community agencies across the valley. Their staff hold office hours in the Student Center, and their forms are posted here so you can print them ahead of time.

Bridgeway Community Services

Bridgeway offers housing navigation, benefits enrollment help, and employment support for adults with disabilities. Bridgeway staff are in the Student Center, Room S-118, on Tuesdays.

Posted for Bridgeway Community Services, a community partner

Bridgeway Community Services — Client Intake Form (Form INT-04)

Bridgeway offers housing navigation, benefits enrollment help, and employment support for adults with disabilities. We welcome everyone! All applicants must complete every section. Incomplete forms will not be processed.

Section 1: Applicant Information

FieldResponse
Full legal name
Preferred name
Date of birth
Address
Phone
Email
Emergency contact (name, relationship, phone)

Section 2: Reason for Contact

Please describe your needs in detail, including any relevant history, previous services received, and the outcome you are seeking. Attach additional pages if necessary.

Section 3: Background

QuestionResponse
Do you have a diagnosed condition? If yes, list all diagnoses and the name of the diagnosing provider.
Are you currently receiving services from another agency? If yes, explain.
Household income (annual)
Have you applied to Bridgeway before? If yes, when and what was the result?

Section 4: Contact and Consent

FieldResponse
Full legal name
Mailing address (if different from above)
Best time to reach you
May we leave a message? (Y/N)

By signing below, I certify that the information provided is true and complete and I authorize Bridgeway Community Services to release and obtain information as necessary in accordance with applicable regulations. Falsification of information may result in termination of services.

Signature: ____________________     Date: __________

Return completed form to the front desk or by mail. Processing times vary.
