Spring Wellness Fair

Office of Student Life · Event flyer

GO CRAZY FOR WELLNESS! Harrow Valley Spring Wellness Fair

Thursday, April 15 · 11 a.m.–3 p.m. · Gymnasium

It's going to be CRAZY fun! Over 50 vendor tables, free smoothies, chair massages, and prize drawings every 15 minutes, announced over the PA system. This is NOT your average health fair, so bring your energy!

  • 12:00 Zumba Blast with a live DJ
  • 1:00 Strobe Light Dance Party (lights off!)
  • 2:00 Lightning Trivia: first to shout the right answer wins!

Get your mind right! Visit the Mental Health table to learn how to beat stress and stop being so OCD about your to-do list. No lame excuses: there's something here for everyone!

Students with special needs should notify staff in advance. Wheelchair users, please use the loading dock entrance at the back of the building.

Doors close at 2:30 for the grand prize raffle. Register online to enter. You must be present to win!
